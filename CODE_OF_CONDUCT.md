# Code of conduct

Faddi is a small project built by people who give their time for free. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, language, country, gender, religion, age or background, and however good their English is.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or who write in a second language.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Explain why, and assume good intent.
- Take feedback on your work gracefully, and give it the way you'd like to receive it.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful for the next person who reads them.

## What isn't okay

- Insults, harassment, threats, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, and anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, tell the maintainer, [@sox29-ui](https://github.com/sox29-ui). When the report should stay private, send it through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/sox29-ui/Faddi/security/advisories/new), which only the maintainer can read. Every report is taken seriously and kept confidential.

## What happens next

Depending on how serious it is, the maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code.
